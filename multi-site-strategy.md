## Quality Criteria (Expanded but Maintained)

### Grade A (High Fit)
- English as working language OR international/global role
- Location: Hong Kong, Singapore, Remote (APAC-compatible)
- Salary: ≥90k RMB/month OR competitive USD
- Role: Product/Program/Strategy Manager
- Company: Fintech, cross-border, AI, logistics tech, major tech

### Grade B (Medium Fit)
- English mentioned but not primary
- Location: Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Shanghai
- Salary: 70-90k RMB/month
- Role: Product/Program/Strategy Manager
- Company: Tech, fintech, e-commerce

### Grade C (Lower Fit)
- No English mentioned
- Location: Any target city
- Salary: ≥70k RMB/month
- Role: Strategic PM/Manager
- Company: Good company, strategic role

### Remove
- <70k RMB/month
- Pure engineering roles
- Hardware/manufacturing
- Chinese-language-only
- Entry-level
- Old postings (>30 days)

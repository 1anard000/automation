# 📈 Weekly Career Strategy — Week of June 15, 2026

## 🔍 Market Analysis

APAC senior PM/strategy hiring is following a "tollbooth" pattern this week: volume is lower than peak scaling periods, but premium roles remain open and are increasingly concentrated in finance infrastructure, sovereign/private capital, and AI-go-to-market functions.

Key trends this week:
- **Strategy and GTM roles remain the strongest segment**, especially APAC/APJC-wide director-level positions.
- **Fintech and payments are still expanding**, with a notable premium on stablecoin and cross-border experience.
- **Asset-management product strategy is hot**: Blackstone, Temasek, GIC, and GM/Senior Associate roles are active across Singapore and Hong Kong.
- **Hiring is still selective**: many roles require English fluency and cross-border expertise; non-English(-friendly) or purely local-ops roles are declining.
- **Entry-level/junior roles remain contracted** in broader tech, reinforcing the need to stay in senior/leadership territory.

## 🎯 Strategy Recommendations

1. **Lead with Singapore and Hong Kong applications.** Singapore has the highest density of A/B-tier senior strategy/PM roles right now. Hong Kong adds the highest upside in fintech and asset-management product roles.

2. **Double down on cross-border finance infrastructure.** Prioritize payments, stablecoin, GTM APAC, and strategic partnerships narrative. This cluster has the clearest active demand.

3. **Develop an explicit AI product narrative.** Amazon and several roles reward clear AI/GTM outcomes, even when the role is not purely technical.

4. **Use quick-apply channels to keep pipeline volume low but steady.** Ashby and standard LinkedIn/Indeed roles are easier to submit and reduce opportunity cost while longer-format roles take more time.

5. **Downgrade purely operational PM roles in this cycle.** The market still rewards strategy and platform leadership over operations-centric titles.

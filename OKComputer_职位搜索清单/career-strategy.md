- **Don't spray-and-pray.** Every application should be targeted and customized.
- **S-tier and A-1 roles get priority.** These are the only ones worth dropping everything for.
- **B-tier roles are "apply if convenient"** — don't spend hours customizing for these.
- **C-tier roles are networking fodder** — use them to build relationships, not as primary targets.

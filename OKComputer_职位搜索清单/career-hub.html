📋 Negotiation

🏆 Comp Negotiation Framework

1
Anchor high, with data.
Use Levels.fyi and Glassdoor data to justify your number. Never name a number first if you can avoid it — let them anchor, then counter 10-20% higher with market data.
2
Negotiate the total package, not just salary.
Equity refresh, sign-on bonus, RSU vesting schedule, remote flexibility, PTO, learning budget. Some levers are easier for them to grant than base.
3
Get competing offers.
Nothing accelerates a decision like "I have another offer at X." Apply broadly, move fast through pipelines. 2-3 competing offers is the sweet spot.
4
Use the "enthusiastic but" technique.
"I'm really excited about this role — and I want to make sure the package reflects the seniority and impact I'll bring." Never negotiate adversarially.
5
Get everything in writing.
Verbal offers aren't offers. Ask for the written offer letter before resigning. Clarify equity terms, cliff, acceleration clauses, and any "gotchas."

## 🎯 Networking Strategy

### Who to Meet at Events
1. **Hiring managers** at target companies (Shopee, ByteDance, Airwallex, etc.)
2. **Other senior PM/strategy people** — potential referrals
3. **Recruiters** specializing in APAC tech
4. **Investors** (if exploring startup path)

### Event Prep Checklist
- [ ] Research attendee list / speaker lineup
- [ ] Identify 3-5 people to connect with
- [ ] Prepare 30-second pitch (cross-border commerce + US-China bridge)
- [ ] Bring business cards or WeChat QR
- [ ] Follow up within 48 hours on LinkedIn/WeChat

### Post-Event Follow-up
- Add contacts to contacts database
- Send personalized follow-up message within 48h
- Schedule coffee chat if mutual interest
- Update outreach tracking

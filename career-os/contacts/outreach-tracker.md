## Follow-up Cadence

### Cold Outreach Sequence
1. **Day 0:** Initial connection request + personalized note
2. **Day 3:** If no response, send follow-up with value add (article, insight)
3. **Day 7:** If no response, try different channel (WeChat if LinkedIn failed)
4. **Day 14:** Final follow-up, offer to stay in touch
5. **Day 30:** Add to long-term nurture list

### Warm Referral Sequence
1. **Day 0:** Thank referrer + ask for intro to target
2. **Day 1:** Send intro email/message to target
3. **Day 3:** Follow up if no response
4. **Day 7:** Try alternative contact method

### Post-Interview Sequence
1. **Same day:** Thank you note (email or WeChat)
2. **Day 2:** Connect on LinkedIn with personalized note
3. **Day 7:** Check in if no update
4. **Day 14:** Follow up on timeline

## Response Templates

### Initial Outreach (LinkedIn)
> Hi [Name], I noticed you're leading [team/initiative] at [Company]. I've been working in cross-border commerce and strategy ops for 9+ years (Amazon, Microsoft, Salesforce) and I'm exploring opportunities in [city]. I'd love to learn more about what you're building. Would you have 15 minutes for a quick chat?

### Follow-up (No Response)
> Hi [Name], just following up on my earlier message. I came across [relevant article/company news] and thought of your work at [Company]. Happy to share if useful. No pressure — just wanted to connect.

### WeChat Initial
> 你好[Name]！我是Ian，在跨境商务和战略运营方面有9年经验（Amazon、Microsoft、Salesforce）。注意到你在[Company]的工作，很想了解一下你们团队在做的事情。方便的话可以聊15分钟吗？

## Part 1: Every Action + Five Forces Assessment

### PHASE 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-4)

#### Action 1.1: Register Wyoming LLC
- **What:** File Wyoming LLC via Stripe Atlas or registered agent (~$200)
- **Why:** App Store requires a business entity; Wyoming is cheapest, most private
- **Five Forces Impact:**
  - New entrants: 🟢 Slightly raises barrier (not everyone will set up an LLC)
  - Supplier power: Neutral
  - Buyer power: Neutral
  - Substitutes: Neutral
  - Rivalry: Neutral
- **Verdict:** Necessary infrastructure, not a moat builder. Do it but don't over-invest.

#### Action 1.2: Get EIN + D-U-N-S Number
- **What:** Apply for IRS EIN (free, takes 2 weeks). Apply for D-U-N-S via Apple (free, takes 1-2 weeks).
- **Why:** Required for Apple Developer Program and Stripe
- **Five Forces Impact:** Neutral across all forces. Pure infrastructure.
- **Verdict:** Required. Start immediately — it takes 2 weeks.

#### Action 1.3: Apple Developer Program ($99/year)
- **What:** Enroll as organization using LLC + D-U-N-S
- **Why:** Required to publish apps on iOS App Store
- **Five Forces Impact:**
  - New entrants: 🟢 Minor barrier ($99 + D-U-N-S + LLC = ~$300 total)
  - Supplier power: 🟢 You control the listing (not the founder)
  - Buyer power: Neutral
  - Substitutes: Neutral
  - Rivalry: Neutral
- **Verdict:** Critical. Controlling the App Store listing is your leverage.

#### Action 1.4: Stripe Account Setup
- **What:** Create Stripe account under LLC. Enable Stripe Connect for split payments.
- **Why:** Handle international payments, automatic rev-share splits
- **Five Forces Impact:**
  - New entrants: 🟢 Minor barrier (Stripe setup is easy but requires LLC)
  - Supplier power: 🟢 You control payment flow (founder can't easily bypass)
  - Buyer power: Neutral
  - Substitutes: Neutral
  - Rivalry: Neutral
- **Verdict:** Important for control. Stripe Connect lets you split payments automatically — founder can't claim they didn't receive their share.

#### Action 1.5: Call with Migaku-like founder
- **What:** 30-min call. Understand: product, users, revenue, international goals, pain points.
- **Why:** Validate the market. Learn what they actually need.
- **Five Forces Impact:**
  - Supplier power: 🟢 Relationship building reduces future supplier power
  - Buyer power: 🟢 Understanding their needs lets you price correctly
  - All others: Neutral
- **Verdict:** Highest-ROI action in Phase 1. One hour of conversation > 10 hours of analysis.

#### Action 1.6: 小红书 Post
- **What:** Post in Chinese: "我是Amazon的产品经理，8年跨境经验。帮中国App做海外市场。免费诊断3个名额。"
- **Why:** Test demand. Get inbound leads.
- **Five Forces Impact:**
  - New entrants: 🟡 If you get traction, others will copy
  - Rivalry: 🟢 First mover in this specific niche
  - All others: Neutral
- **Verdict:** Low effort, high signal. Tells you if the market exists.

#### Action 1.7: Join WeChat 出海 Groups

- **Why:** Pipeline source + market intelligence
- **Five Forces Impact:**

## Part 2: Rev Share vs Fee Model

### Rev Share Model

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Aligned incentives (you earn more if app grows) | Founder can cut you out when app succeeds |
| Lower upfront cost for founder | Hard to enforce cross-border |
| Potential upside if app explodes | Unpredictable income |
| You control the App Store listing | Complex accounting |

**When rev share makes sense:** Only when you control the App Store listing AND the founder can't easily go around you. This means: iOS-only apps where you own the developer account.

### Fee Model (Monthly Retainer)

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Predictable income | No upside if app explodes |
| Simple invoicing | Founder sees it as a cost |
| You're paid for your time | Income capped by hours |
| Easy to enforce | Less aligned incentives |

**When fee model makes sense:** Always. It's simpler, more predictable, and easier to sell.

### Hybrid Model (Recommended)

**Base fee + performance bonus:**

| Component | Amount | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Base retainer | ¥8,000/month | Guaranteed |
| Performance bonus | ¥2,000 | Hit 100 new Western users/month |
| Performance bonus | ¥5,000 | Hit 500 new Western users/month |
| Performance bonus | ¥10,000 | Hit 1,000 new Western users/month |

**Why this works:**
- Founder has predictable costs (¥8k base)
- You're incentivized to drive results (bonuses)
- If the app takes off, you share in the upside (but via fees, not equity)
- No messy rev-share accounting
- No risk of being cut out (you're a service provider, not a partner)

**Verdict:** Use the hybrid model. Rev share is too risky and hard to enforce. Fees with performance bonuses give you upside without the structural weaknesses.

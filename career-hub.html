💡 Positioning & Strategy
Unique Value Prop
Cross-border e-commerce × AI GTM × APAC expertise
This combination is rare. Most PMs have one of these. Having all three — deep cross-border ops experience, AI product launch track record, and APAC market knowledge — positions for Director+ roles at companies expanding internationally.
Target Role Profile
Sweet spot: Director/Head of Product or Strategy at a fintech or cross-border commerce company expanding in APAC. Singapore or Shenzhen base.
Stretch: VP/GM at a Series B+ startup entering APAC. Higher risk, higher upside.
Safe: Senior PM at a large tech company (Airwallex, Stripe, Shopee). Good comp, less leverage.

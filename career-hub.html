Strategy Playbook

How to Stand Out in APAC PM Applications

Generic, actionable advice — no personal details, just what works in this market.

01
📄
Resume Format
  • One page for APAC markets — conciseness is valued
  • Lead with impact metrics (GMV, revenue, conversion)
  • Use reverse chronological — standard for CN/HK/SG
  • Include both English and Chinese versions for mainland roles
  • Highlight cross-border and multi-market experience prominently
02
🎤
Interview Prep
  • Prepare 3-5 case studies with quantified results
  • Practice market sizing — popular in SG/HK interviews
  • Know the local competitive landscape for each market
  • Be ready to discuss AI integration in product strategy
  • For mainland China: expect cultural fit questions alongside technical
03
💰
Salary Negotiation
  • CN: Negotiate total package (base + equity + bonus)
  • HK: Ask about MPF contributions and bonus structure
  • SG: Factor in CPF and variable pay components
  • Cross-border: Relocation packages are common — ask early
  • Research market rates using Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, and local platforms
04
🌐
Network Strategically
  • Join ProductTank and local PM meetups
  • Attend chamber of commerce events (AmCham, BritCham, EuroCham)
  • Engage on LinkedIn with APAC product leaders
  • Use referrals — they matter more in APAC than you'd think
  • Follow VC firms in the region for portfolio company hiring
05
🏢
Company Research
  • Understand the org structure before interviewing
  • Research the CEO/founder's public statements
  • Check Glassdoor and 脉脉 (Maimai) for culture signals
  • Know the funding stage and recent valuation
  • For Chinese companies: understand the 汇报线 (reporting line)
06
📍
Location Strategy
  • Singapore — strongest for English-friendly, international roles
  • Hong Kong — finance, crypto, and regional HQs
  • Shenzhen — hardware + e-commerce + fast-scaling startups
  • Shanghai — consulting, MNC HQs, fintech
  • Remote from APAC — increasingly viable for senior roles
